wlanscout+ flasher v@VERSION@

Writes wlanscout+ to an ESP32-C5 through its download mode. Plug the board's USB-C port marked USB (not UART) into the phone. It needs no button; if it does not connect, hold BOOT and press RESET, then try again. The same gets a board back whose firmware no longer starts.
